The CSV files labeled attempt are small treesequences that I made up. 
The CSV files labeled test are treesequences that Halley made while testing extend haplotypes. 
The CSV files with sigma and rep listed are from large slimulated treesequences. 

Generally speaking when comparing only unary nodes it does seem that extending haplotypes does increase accuracy (however only slightly) in the majority of cases. 

Based on my testing, it seems there are two main cases with the largest difference in error. One is that nodes that originally existed only on a small portion of the genome but were extended across a much larger portion of it seem to have a more significant drop in error. The other (which I am still trying to come up with a treesequence to test more directly for) is it seems there is also a more significant drop in error in nodes in regions where the topography changed significantly during extension.
